## Introduction

Recently, I've been interested in moving to [CachyOS](https://wiki.cachyos.org/) from Arch Linux.
Why? No reason at all, but what is important is that this would be my first distro-hop.

When I first installed Arch, I did so from my Windows boot, and
I bought a completely fresh SSD solely for it.
As such, I barely had to do any pre-install steps.
I simply had to delete the newly added SSD from my Windows partitions, and
the Arch installation script was happy to eat up the freed up space!

This helped me avoid many problems I might have had otherwise, such as
backing up pre-existing data on the drive.
Unfortunately, this also meant that I glossed over a lot of the actions
I did in the process of installing Arch.
After all, I had nothing to lose by blindly following instructions.

## What's Going On?

Just wanted to say that I now have Arch on my desktop.
Rah!!!

## Secret Purpose

In case you didn't know, I love video games. Maybe a little too much.
Every time I do work on my home PC, the temptation
of launching [DoS2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/435150/Divinity_Original_Sin_2__Definitive_Edition/) calls to me like the Green Goblin mask.
This was a problem as not only was it a major distraction,
it eventually conditioned me to become unable to do work at home.
With the workload of studies and projects building up,
I decided it was time to stop being a bum.

The solution is simple.
Dual-boot Arch and Windows, using Arch like a work profile.
If I wanted to game, I would have to boot into Windows to do so.
Hopefully, the thought of doing so would inconvenience me enough to
continue working instead.
So far, this has been successful!

## Final Thoughts

If you currently struggle focusing on work at home,
try separating your home and work environments!
I'm not saying you have to install Arch like I did, but
something like logging into a new user profile may put you in
the right mindset.

For those of you that have problems focusing on work at home,
do you do something similar? Let me know!

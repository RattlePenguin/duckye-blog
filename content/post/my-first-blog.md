## Introduction

I wanted to start a blog, so I started one.
This is my first blog post, and I want to
use this chance to pave the way of my blogging journey.
I always thought it corny to write out my thoughts and show them to the world,
but maybe this is something I need right now.

No one may see this, or maybe I'll become famous and everyone will see this.

When interviewers ask me "What is your biggest weakness?"
I always tell them: "I wait for a perfect idea that will never come."
I never make something new because someone else has done it better.
I waste time learning best practices when what I know already works.
I never finish a project because I think it's too messy, or slow.
I'm sick of waiting. This blog is my commitment to accepting small wins.

## Goals

- Blog as much as possible. Don't be afraid of what others think.
- Share my blog with others. I don't want this to be a diary,
I want to show others who I am and what I've done!

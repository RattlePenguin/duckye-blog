My First Blog

Welcome to my blog!

Published Sun, Aug 9, 2026 by Benedict Setiawan

Introduction

I wanted to start a blog, so I started one. This is my first blog post, and I want to use this chance to pave the way of my blogging journey. I always thought it corny to write out my thoughts and show them to the world, but maybe this is something I need right now.

Whenever interviewers ask me “What is your biggest weakness?” I always tell them: “I wait for a perfect idea that will never come.” I never make something new because someone else has done it better. I waste time learning best practices when what I know already works. I never finish a project because I think it’s too messy, or slow. I’m sick of waiting. This blog is my commitment to accepting small wins. If I’m going to create something that I am truly proud of, I need to be comfortable with failure.

From now, I’m just going to write whatever I want, however I want. And you my dear reader, can tag along for the ride! Who knows, maybe no one will ever see this, or I may become famous and this will be on the headlines.

Yeet.

Goals

  • Blog as much as possible. Don’t be afraid of what others think.
  • Share my blog with others. I don’t want this to be a diary, I want to show others who I am and what I’ve done.
  • Review my blog posts after some time. Setting and forgetting thoughts is fine, but come back to remember why you wrote in the first place.
